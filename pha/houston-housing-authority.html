Houston Section 8 NSPIRE Inspection Guide:
Houston Housing Authority (HHA)

Landlords and property managers participating in the Houston Housing Authority (HHA) Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) program are subject to HUD's NSPIRE inspection framework. If your property receives a 24-hour Life-Threatening (LT) citation, Houston Housing Authority (HHA) is federally mandated to place your Housing Assistance Payment (HAP) voucher subsidy into immediate abatement.

Top 5 Automatic Inspection Failures in Houston Units

Inspectors dispatched across Houston strictly cite these 5 items during annual and change-of-tenancy NSPIRE audits:

1

Smoke Alarm Battery Tamper-Resistance

Battery-only smoke alarms must feature a sealed 10-year tamper-resistant lithium battery. Alarms requiring replacement 9V batteries are an automatic failure in sleeping rooms and hallways.

Mandatory 24-Hour Cure Notice
2

GFCI Protection Within 6 Feet of Water

All receptacles within 6 feet of kitchen sink rims, bathroom vanities, or laundry basins must be GFCI protected and trip immediately when the test button is depressed.

Mandatory 24-Hour Cure Notice
3

Water Heater TPR Relief Discharge Line

The Temperature and Pressure Relief (TPR) safety discharge pipe must be rigid metal (copper) or CPVC and terminate between 2 and 6 inches from the floor surface.

Mandatory 24-Hour Cure Notice
4

Bedroom Emergency Egress Window Operation

At least one window in every bedroom must open easily without tools and remain suspended without physical props or weights to allow emergency firefighter access.

Mandatory 24-Hour Cure Notice
5

Interior Keyless Entry Lock Deadbolts

Double-cylinder locks requiring an interior key to exit are strictly prohibited under federal life-safety codes. Only thumb-turn deadbolts are permitted on exterior egress doors.

Mandatory 24-Hour Cure Notice
