## 🔍 How You Can Help Us Prepare Your Home
Please take a few moments to review this simple checklist before the inspector arrives:

* [ ] **Clear All Bedroom Windows**: Federal rules require bedroom windows to open easily in case of emergency. Please move any beds, dressers, toys, or boxes away from all bedroom windows so the inspector can test them.
* [ ] **Check Smoke & Carbon Monoxide Alarms**: Never disconnect, cover, or remove batteries from smoke alarms. If any alarm in your home is beeping or chirping, please notify management immediately so we can replace it for free today!
* [ ] **Keep Entryways & Hallways Clear**: Please ensure front and back entry doors, hallways, and staircases are completely clear of bikes, boxes, strollers, or trash.
* [ ] **Report Leaks Under Sinks**: If you have any dripping faucets, water pooling under sinks, or a running toilet, let us know today so our maintenance team can fix it before the inspector arrives.
* [ ] **Secure Household Pets**: All pets must be safely secured in a crate or removed from the home during the inspection window to ensure the inspector can safely enter all rooms.
* [ ] **Clear Storage Around Utility Equipment**: If your water heater or furnace is located inside a closet in your unit, all personal storage, clothes, and boxes must be cleared away from the equipment.

---

## ❓ What Will The Inspector Do?
The inspector will walk through all rooms of your home. They will test your light switches, stove burners, window latches, smoke detectors, and sink faucets. The walkthrough typically takes only **10 to 15 minutes**.
