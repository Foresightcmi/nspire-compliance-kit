# 🚨 THE TOP 25 IMMEDIATE HUD NSPIRE FAILURES
### Plain-English Emergency Guide for Landlords, Maintenance Staff & Property Owners
*Written for everyday property owners. No legal jargon. Clear, simple instructions.*

---

## ⚡ The 24-Hour Rule: What You Must Know
Under HUD's **NSPIRE** rules, defects are split into categories. The most dangerous category is **Life-Threatening (LT)**. 
* If a federal inspector finds ANY item on this list, you receive a **mandatory 24-Hour Emergency Repair Notice**.
* If you do not fix it and upload photo proof to HUD within 24 hours, **HUD can immediately stop paying your monthly Section 8 rent voucher checks**.

Use this simple guide to walk your property before the inspector arrives.

### 1. Smoke Alarms (The #1 Most Common Fail)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Every bedroom, the hallway outside bedrooms, and every level of the home must have a working smoke alarm.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: 
  * Missing smoke alarm.
  * Alarm chirps or battery is dead.
  * Alarm has removable 9V batteries instead of a sealed 10-year tamper-resistant battery (if battery-only unit).
  * Painted over or covered with plastic.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Buy sealed 10-year lithium battery smoke alarms (\$15 at Home Depot/Lowe's). Install one inside each bedroom and one in the main hallway. Push the test button until it beeps.

---

### 2. Carbon Monoxide (CO) Alarms
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Required in any home with gas appliances (gas stove, gas furnace, gas water heater), an attached garage, or a fireplace.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: No CO alarm on each level or outside the immediate vicinity of the bedrooms.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Plug a combination Smoke/CO alarm into an outlet or mount on the wall in the hallway outside bedrooms.

---

### 3. Kitchen & Bathroom Outlets (GFCI Protection)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Every electrical outlet within 6 feet of any water source (kitchen sink, bathroom sink, laundry tub, wet bar).
* 🚨 **What Fails**: 
  * A regular outlet with no "TEST" and "RESET" buttons within 6 feet of water.
  * A GFCI outlet that does not click and cut off power when the "TEST" button is pressed.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Replace regular receptacles within 6 feet of water with a 15-Amp GFCI outlet (\$12 part, 10-minute install by handyman).

---

### 4. Water Heater Safety Discharge Pipe (TPR Valve)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: The copper or CPVC safety pipe coming off the side/top of the water heater tank.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * Pipe is missing completely.
  * Pipe ends more than 6 inches above the floor.
  * Pipe points upward or is capped/plugged.
  * Pipe made of flexible plastic garden hose or illegal PVC.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Ensure a rigid 3/4" copper or CPVC pipe runs down the side of the tank and terminates between **2 inches and 6 inches** above the floor (or points directly into a floor drain).

---

### 5. Bedroom Windows & Emergency Exit (Egress)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: At least one window in every bedroom must open easily so someone can escape a fire.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * Window is painted shut or nailed shut.
  * Window does not stay open by itself (broken sash cord).
  * Heavy window A/C unit blocks the only opening window in the bedroom.
  * Security bars on the window that require a key to open from the inside.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Scrape away dried paint so the window glides open smoothly. Fix sash cords or install a \$5 window support spring. Move window A/C units to non-egress windows. Ensure security bars have quick-release handles.

---

### 6. Door Locks (Deadbolts)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Exterior entry doors.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: **Double-cylinder deadbolts** (locks that require a key from the INSIDE to unlock). In a fire, people get trapped looking for keys.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Replace double-cylinder locks with standard thumb-turn deadbolts. You must be able to turn the knob and walk out without needing a key.

---

### 7. Electrical Panels (Missing Knockout Plugs)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: The main breaker box.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * Missing breaker cover (exposed live metal bus bars).
  * Open round holes ("knockouts") on the side or face of the box where a finger could touch wires.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Buy \$1 plastic or metal electrical knockout snap-in seals from the hardware store and pop them into any empty holes.

---

### 8. Exposed Electrical Wires & Missing Plates
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Light switches, wall outlets, ceiling junction boxes.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: Cracked switch plates, open light sockets with exposed bare copper wires, or open ceiling boxes.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Install 79¢ plastic faceplates over every open box. Cap any loose wires with wire nuts inside an enclosed box.

---

### 9. Gas Stove Burners & Odors
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Gas ranges and oven burners.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * Any smell of raw gas.
  * A burner clicks repeatedly but fails to ignite flame within 5 seconds.
  * Oven door does not shut tightly.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Clean grease and food crumbs off burner pilot igniters. If gas smells are detected, call the gas utility company or a licensed plumber immediately.

---

### 10. Stove Anti-Tip Bracket
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: The metal safety bracket installed on the floor behind the kitchen stove.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: When stepping gently on the open oven door, the stove tilts forward (crush hazard for small children).
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Install a universal \$8 anti-tip metal bracket into the baseboard or floor and slide the rear stove foot into it.

---

### 11. Handrails & Guardrails (Fall Hazards)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Stairs and elevated porches.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * Any interior or exterior staircase with **4 or more steps** missing a sturdy handrail.
  * Loose handrail that wobbles when grabbed.
  * Any porch, balcony, or deck higher than 30 inches off the ground without a 36-inch guardrail.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Tighten mounting screws into wall studs. If missing, install an inexpensive handrail and brackets before inspection day.

---

### 12. Tripping Hazards on Walkways
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Front sidewalks, driveways, entry steps, and interior transition strips.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: Any crack, raised concrete edge, or damaged floor transition that sticks up **3/4 of an inch or more**.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Grind down raised concrete sidewalk edges or patch with quick-set cement ramp. Secure loose carpet or floor transition strips.

---

### 13. Clothes Dryer Exhaust Vent
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: The vent hose connecting the dryer to the outside wall.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * White thin vinyl/plastic flexible accordion hose (fire hazard).
  * Vent exhausting into the crawlspace, attic, or garage instead of outdoors.
  * Damaged, crushed, or disconnected vent.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Replace with rigid or semi-rigid aluminum metal ducting (\$12 part). Ensure it blows air to the outdoors and the outside vent flap opens freely.

---

### 14. Water Leaks & Mold-Like Substances
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Under kitchen and bathroom sinks, ceilings, around toilets.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * Active dripping water under sinks.
  * Soft, spongy floor around the base of a toilet (leaking wax ring).
  * Visible black or green mold-like substance larger than 1 square foot on drywall or ceiling.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Tighten P-trap slip nuts under sinks. Replace toilet wax ring (\$5 part). Clean any surface mildew with detergent/water and fix the underlying leak.

---

### 15. Blocked Hallways & Fire Egress
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Front doors, back doors, main hallways, and stairwells.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: Storage boxes, old furniture, bikes, or trash blocking doors or narrowing hallways to less than 36 inches wide.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Notify tenants in advance to remove all storage and clutter from entryways and exit pathways.

---

### 16. Peeling & Flaking Paint (Pre-1978 Homes)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Interior and exterior walls, window sills, doors, and trim on homes built before 1978.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: Any chipping, peeling, or chalking paint accessible to children (Lead-Based Paint hazard).
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Wet-scrape loose paint, vacuum with a HEPA filter, and seal with a fresh coat of primer and exterior/interior paint. Never dry-sand old paint.

---

### 17. Infestation (Roaches, Mice, Bedbugs)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Under sinks, behind refrigerators, kitchen cabinets.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: Live roaches, mouse droppings, or active bedbug evidence.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Hire a pest control operator for a targeted treatment at least one week prior to the inspection. Clean all cabinet bases and droppings thoroughly.

---

### 18. Call-for-Aid Emergency Pull Cords (Senior Housing)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Senior or disabled housing units with emergency cords in bathrooms and bedrooms.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * The cord is tied up in a knot, cut short, or wrapped around the towel bar.
  * The cord does not hang within **6 inches of the floor** (so a fallen person can reach it).
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Untie all cords. If short, tie an extension cord string so it hangs 4 to 6 inches above the finished floor.

---

### 19. Fire Extinguishers (Multifamily & Common Areas)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Fire extinguishers in hallways or inside units.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * Gauge needle is in the red zone (discharged or over-pressurized).
  * Missing inspection service tag or inspection is expired (past 1 year).
  * Missing lock pin.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Check the pressure gauge. If not in the green, replace or recharge. Have local fire equipment service tag the extinguisher.

---

### 20. Heating System (Minimum Heat Guarantee)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Furnace, baseboard heaters, or heat pump.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: The heating system cannot maintain a minimum temperature of **68°F** in living spaces during cold weather.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Service the furnace, replace dirty air filters, and ensure thermostat batteries are fresh.

---

### 21. Refrigerator Door Gasket (Food Spoilage)
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: The rubber seal around the refrigerator and freezer doors.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: Torn rubber gasket that lets cold air escape; refrigerator cannot keep food below 40°F.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Clean or replace damaged magnetic door gaskets (\$25 part). Ensure the refrigerator thermostat is set properly.

---

### 22. Inoperable Interior Doors & Bath Privacy
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Bedroom and bathroom doors.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * Bathroom door has no working latch or lock for privacy.
  * Door rubs so hard on the floor or frame that it cannot close and latch shut.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Tighten hinge screws or plane down sticking door edges so the door latches with a simple push.

---

### 23. Flammable Materials Stored Near Heating Units
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Utility closets containing gas furnaces or water heaters.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: Paint cans, gasoline, cardboard boxes, mattresses, or clothes stored directly against or within 3 feet of a gas-fired heater.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Completely clear the furnace/water heater closet. Post a sign: "Utility Room — No Storage Allowed."

---

### 24. Missing or Broken Sinks & Toilets
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Faucets, sink basins, toilet stability.
* 🚨 **What Fails**:
  * Cracked porcelain with sharp razor edges.
  * Toilet rocks back and forth when sat upon (loose closet bolts).
  * Sink faucet does not produce hot and cold running water.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Tighten toilet floor bolts. Replace chipped basins or broken toilet seats.

---

### 25. Roof Leaks & Ceiling Water Stains
* 🔍 **What The Inspector Checks**: Ceilings in top-floor units and exterior roofline.
* 🚨 **What Fails**: Sagging drywall, active leaks, or dark water stains on ceilings that test wet with a moisture meter.
* 🛠️ **How to Fix It**: Repair roof shingles or flashing. Once dry, seal ceiling stains with stain-blocking primer (Kilz) and repaint.
